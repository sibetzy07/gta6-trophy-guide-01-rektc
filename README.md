An offline guide and tracker for all 55 Grand Theft Auto VI trophies on PlayStation 5. Every trophy keeps its official name, description and grade as PlayStation publishes them, with a guide in our own words and its cited source beside it (Push Square, Output Lag and Sixshore), a six-step roadmap to the platinum with the one missable trophy flagged, and a checklist that saves your progress in the browser, filters by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, online), hides story spoilers until a trophy is earned, and exports and imports your progress as a file. The whole thing runs from its own files — no network requests, no Rockstar artwork.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080
