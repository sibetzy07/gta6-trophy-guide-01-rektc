Read this first — the one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy that can be missed: Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 disappears once that chapter ends. Do it in Chapter 1, and keep a manual save from the start of every chapter plus one untouched save from just before the final mission — there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus.

Never use cheats on your trophy save: they switch off trophies and saving. There are no online trophies, no difficulty trophies and no mission medals.
